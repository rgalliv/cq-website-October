# CraneQualified: Build your next generation.

Review draft - October 14, 2026. Confirm scope and readiness before external use.

Specialist development for crane and rigging employers.

## Develop the people you already employ

Make role requirements, development gaps, the next step and the evidence behind employer decisions visible. Connect structured learning, supervised practice and observed performance around the work.

## Run it with your team

Your coordinator and supervisors run the agreed pathway: learning assignments, workplace practice, feedback, evidence collection and employer reviews. Preserve your approved training providers and original credential sources.

## Or have us manage it with you

Proposed coordination scope: one existing employer-accepted pathway, roster and evidence inventory, development follow-up, gap reporting and a monthly manager review. Confirm accepted content, responsibilities and delivery readiness before starting.

## Scope specialist work separately

Training delivery, custom curriculum, site visits, travel, practical evaluations, external exams and certification fees require separate scope and pricing. Existing implementation or evaluation pilots remain separate offers.

## Keep the evidence and decisions distinct

Learning, supervised practice, observed competence, external certification, employer qualification and task authorization are different records. Credentials retain their issuing bodies. Course completion alone does not authorize work.

## Practical learning. Employer authority.

Use realistic scenarios, retrieval practice, spaced refreshers, feedback and attributable observations. Your employer remains responsible for supervision, qualification and work authorization; no credential, qualification or retention outcome is guaranteed.
